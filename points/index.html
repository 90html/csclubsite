    How points work

    Show up. Solve. Compete.

    Every meeting, contest and problem counts toward your total.

    7points / semester

    Unlocks the end-of-semester (EOS) party

    Hit the goal each semester to get in. We also reward our most devoted members with pizza parties.

    Ways to earn

    • Meetings

      Answer questions at meetings. The more you get right, the more points you earn.

      Varies

    • Competitions

      Compete in contests like UIL invitationals to earn points.

      Varies

    • Problem of the Week

      Solve the POTWs posted in the club Discord server.

      0.5 each · max 2

    • LeetCode

      Solve LeetCode problems. Don't just peek at the solution!

      0.5 each · max 1

    • Codeforces & online contests

      Compete in Codeforces rounds or other online competitions. No limit, so go crazy.

      1 each · no limit

    • USACO

      0.5 points for each attempt, plus 0.5 for every problem where you pass at least half the test cases.

      0.5 + 0.5/problem · no limit

    • CS memes

      DM CS memes (picture or reel) to the club Instagram.

      5 memes = 0.5 · max 0.5

    Demerits are in effect this year. Please act responsibly at meetings and events.
